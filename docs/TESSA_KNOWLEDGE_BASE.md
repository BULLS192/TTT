## Audio

### audio-speakers-only

**Typical questions:** will new speakers make my car sound better · is replacing speakers enough · can i just upgrade the speakers

**Answer:** Speakers can help, but factory processing, available power, mounting and the incoming signal can limit the result. TTT looks at the complete signal path before assuming speakers alone are the best first step.

**Follow-up:** What vehicle and factory audio system do you have?

**Mode:** qualify · **Service:** Audio & DSP

### audio-dsp

**Typical questions:** do i need a dsp · what does a dsp do · why use a dsp in car audio

**Answer:** A DSP can control routing, crossover, equalization, delay and level structure. Not every build requires one, but it is especially useful when factory processing or a more precise tune is involved.

### audio-clipping

**Typical questions:** what is amplifier clipping · why do speakers distort when loud · can clipping damage speakers

**Answer:** Clipping occurs when an amplifier or signal stage is driven beyond its clean output capability. It can sound harsh and increase thermal stress, so correct gain structure and system tuning matter.

**Mode:** answer · **Service:** Audio & DSP

### audio-oem-eq

**Typical questions:** what is factory equalization · why is factory signal hard to integrate · does my factory radio change eq with volume

**Answer:** Many factory audio systems apply equalization, filtering, all-pass processing or volume-dependent behavior before the signal reaches the speakers. Good integration measures or accounts for that processing instead of treating every speaker wire as a flat source.

### audio-bass-restoration

**Typical questions:** what is bass rolloff · why does factory radio lose bass at high volume · can dsp fix bass rolloff

**Answer:** Some factory systems reduce low-frequency output as volume rises to protect factory speakers. Integration hardware or DSP strategy can compensate where appropriate, but the original signal should be measured first.

**Mode:** answer · **Service:** Audio & DSP
